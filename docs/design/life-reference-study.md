## Applied to Life
- White inset cards on quiet neutral surfaces; structured KPI headers and fine dividers.
- Rounded gradient chart bars, ghost tracks, contextual hover/focus labels, and actual recorded counts.
- Category dashboards share one consistent layout with scoped records, activity and stage distributions.
- Separate document bank, visible entry point, direct status changes, compact forms.
- Retain the existing blue primary buttons; continue using the licensed rounded Ionicons assets.
- No invented trends, revenue or application outcomes.
