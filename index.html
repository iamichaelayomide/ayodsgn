FAQ

What people are asking

What is your typical project timeline?

Most brand-led websites and product interface designs take between 2 to 4 weeks from discovery to final handoff. Timeline depends on scope and asset readiness.

Do you build the websites you design?

Yes, I develop lightweight, production-ready static pages, frontend components, and CMS integrations. For complex apps, I hand over clean design tokens and CSS details to engineering teams.

What tools and platforms do you use?

I design in Figma and build using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Next.js, or Webflow. I focus on clean setups that stay easy to maintain and scale after launch.

How do we get started?

Book a brief call through the contact form. We will review your project goals, scope, and timeline to make sure it is a great fit, then outline the next steps.
